About SafeOn

SafeOn is a startup focused on building smart wearable safety technology for students, women, senior citizens and professionals.

Our flagship product, SafeWalk AI Sensor, is a compact AI-enabled wearable that provides instant emergency assistance without depending on constantly using a mobile phone.

The sensor is lightweight, waterproof, rechargeable, and can be attached to a bag, keychain, ID card, belt, or clothing.

Key Features

🆘 One Press SOS

Press the emergency button once to instantly send an alert along with your live location to your trusted contacts.

Continuously shares the user's live location so family members can track and reach them quickly.

Automatically records surrounding audio after the SOS button is activated for better emergency support.
